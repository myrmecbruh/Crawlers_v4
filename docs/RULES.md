# Rules

## Traits
- [gospel] Six traits: Might, Agility, Endurance, Presence, Intellect, Willpower.
- [gospel] Traits run 1 to 3.
- [slop] Might: raw force. Agility: speed and fine control. Endurance: staying power. Presence: effect on others. Intellect: what can be learned and worked out. Willpower: carrying on anyway.
- [slop] Practice never raises a trait. Only rare events (a scar, a card) change one.

## Skills
- [gospel] Skills run 0 to 3.
- [gospel] Skills are per material or tool: the skill follows what you work with (stone, wood, metal, blade); the trait follows the action.
- [gospel] Work rolls use the skill of the material being worked (mining is Might + Stone). Combat rolls use the weapon's tool skill (Might + Blade).
- [slop] Material skills: Stone, Wood, Metal, Fiber, Flesh (hide, bone, meat, bodies), Plant (crops, herbs, soil), Relic (old machinery).
- [slop] Tool skills: Blade, Haft (clubs, hammers, picks, axes), Bow, Shield.
- [slop] A task names one action (which gives the trait) and one material or tool (which gives the skill). Example: mining granite is Might + Stone; carving a stone idol is Agility + Stone; setting a broken leg is Intellect + Flesh; swinging a sword is Might + Blade.

## The roll
- [gospel] Roll a pool of d6: trait + skill dice. Each 5 or 6 is a success.
- [slop] A task's difficulty is the number of successes it needs, 1 to 3.
- [slop] Dice are drawn on screen at the place the roll happens.
- [slop] Tools, materials, wounds, needs and bonds add or remove whole dice. Nothing ever changes the 5+ target.

## Fail forward
- [gospel] A failed roll never stalls the task: it still makes progress, with a complication (lost token, injury, time, noise).
- [gospel] Failures are the only source of skill marks. Enough marks raise the skill by 1.
- [slop] Marks to reach skill 1 / 2 / 3: 2 / 4 / 6.
- [slop] Complication is picked by the task: crafting loses an input token or makes a flawed item; gathering takes longer; dungeon work makes noise; fighting takes a wound.

## Needs
- [gospel] Six needs, each a small 0 to 6 track: Hunger, Rest, Temperature, Wounds, Stress (morale), Relationships.
- [gospel] Each need is resisted by exactly one trait.
- [gospel] Hunger: Might. Temperature: Agility. Wounds: Endurance. Rest: Intellect. Stress: Willpower. Relationships: Presence.
- [slop] When a need track is at its worst, the colonist rolls the resisting trait each day; failure removes a die from all pools until the need is met.
- [gospel] Hunger is met by eating food tokens; Rest by sleeping in a bed.
- [gospel] Stress is raised by death, filth and blight and lowered by comfort; a break causes a bad event.
- [gospel] Relationships are bonds and grudges between colonists that add or remove dice when they work together.
- [slop] Temperature comes from season, weather card, depth, nearby fire tokens and worn gear.

## Wounds and death
- [gospel] Health is wounds per body area, tied to the six worn slots, not hit points.
- [gospel] A defeated character is downed first, can be carried home to recover with a lasting scar, and dies only if abandoned.
- [slop] Each area takes up to 2 wounds. A wounded area removes a die from actions that use it. A third wound anywhere downs the character.
- [slop] Armour on the hit area rolls its material's hardness in dice; each success cancels a wound.
- [slop] A corpse and its gear stay in the world as tokens.

## Equipment
- [gospel] Six worn slots, each visible on the sprite: Head, Torso, Legs, Feet, Hands, Mantle (neck, back, shoulders).
- [gospel] Two hands, separate from the six: each holds one thing (weapon, tool, shield or a resource token).
- [gospel] A pack adds a few visible carried tokens.
- [slop] A pack is a Mantle item; capacity 2 to 4 tokens, drawn on the character's back.
- [slop] Held items are also drawn on the sprite.

## Materials
- [gospel] Every item, object and resource has a material tag.
- [gospel] A material has a small stat line (2 to 3 small numbers such as hardness, weight, worth), inherited by anything made of it, plus its colour on the sprite.
- [gospel] A material also has keyword tags (flammable, conductive, holy, brittle) that rules and cards check.
- [slop] Stats run 1 to 3.
- [slop] Families: metal, stone, wood, fiber, hide, bone, plant, relic. Each family has a handful of named materials.

## Combat
- [gospel] Combat is automatic, on the same slow ticks as everything else. The party fights by itself; dice pools are rolled visibly each exchange.
- [slop] An exchange is one attack roll against the target's defence successes; the margin is wounds dealt.
- [gospel] The player has a recall bell: one button, the party drops its goal and heads home. Nothing finer than that.
- [slop] A party also turns back by its own judgement.
