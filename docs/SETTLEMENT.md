# Settlement

## Tokens
- [gospel] Every resource is a token visible in the world. There is no abstract stockpile count.
- [slop] A tile holds a stack of up to 6 tokens, all drawn.
- [slop] Colonists move tokens by carrying them, one per hand plus pack.

## Food
- [gospel] Foraging and hunting: Plant and Flesh tokens gathered from the plot and beyond.
- [gospel] Farming: voxel fields; crops are tokens that grow in place over days, by season.
- [gospel] Cooking: a station turns raw tokens into meals that feed more or lift Stress; raw food is risky.
- [gospel] Spoilage: food tokens rot after some days unless stored cold, salted or smoked.

## Stations
- [gospel] Each built station takes visible input tokens and a colonist's roll, and outputs tokens (ore to bar to tool). Chains are the engine.
- [slop] Inputs sit on the station's in-tile, outputs appear on its out-tile.
- [slop] A station is built from tokens and takes its material from them.

## Devices
- [gospel] Later, simple automation (chutes, carts, water wheels) moves or works tokens without a colonist.
- [slop] A device does one fixed thing per tick or per several ticks and never rolls dice.

## Cards
- [gospel?] Cards are probably the main prize of expeditions.
- [gospel?] They go into a deck that is drawn from daily, for engine building in the manner of Dominion.
- [gospel?] Cards may control boons and banes such as weather, disease, crops and raids.
- [gospel] How the daily draw works is undecided; it is on the roadmap.
- [gospel] Deck shaping: cards have a small use count shown as pips (banes expire, boons wear out), and a shrine station destroys a card for a token cost and a roll.
- [slop] One deck. The day's draw is laid face up on the interface side and applies until the next dawn.
- [slop] The starting deck is mostly banes and blanks; expedition cards dilute it with boons.
